He bowed and left his household. Tian Dao said, Be my attendant, and from now on I will teach you the essential Dharma gate.

After one year passed, Long Tan said, When I arrived, you said that you would teach me the essential Dharma gate. I haven't received any of your instruction as yet. Tian Dao said, I have been teaching you for a long time. Long Tan said, What have you been teaching me? Tian Dao said, When you greet me, I join my palms. When I sit, you stand beside me. When you bring me tea, I receive it from you.

Long Tan was silent for a while. Tian Dao said, When you see it, you just see it. When you think about it, you miss it. Long Tan then had great enlightenment.
